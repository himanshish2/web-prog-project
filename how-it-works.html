THE JOURNEY

Request → Accept → Connect → Deliver → Earn

Getting your parcel delivered across campus is just four simple steps.

01

Create a Request

Need something delivered? Enter the pickup and drop-off locations, parcel details, preferred time and the reward you are offering.

Once submitted, your request becomes visible to Campus Runners who are available to complete the delivery.

→

02

Get Matched with a Runner

A fellow BMU student browsing available requests accepts your delivery and becomes your Campus Runner.

Once accepted, the request status is updated and you can view the runner and accepted request details.

→

03

Connect & Collect

Coordinate with your Campus Runner through chat, confirm the pickup details and arrange a convenient handover for the parcel.

The runner collects the parcel from the specified pickup location and updates its status so you can follow the delivery progress.

→

04

Track, Deliver & Earn

Track your parcel until it reaches its destination. Once delivered, the requester receives the parcel and the Campus Runner earns the offered reward.

The runner marks the parcel as delivered, completing the request. A simple campus delivery that benefits both students!
