About CampusCarry

CampusCarry is a student-to-student parcel delivery platform designed to make parcel collection easier within campus. Students often receive parcels when they are attending classes, busy with work, or unable to reach the collection point. CampusCarry connects them with other students who are already travelling around campus and can collect and deliver the parcel for them.

Rewards & Commission
