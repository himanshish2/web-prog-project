About CampusCarry

CampusCarry is a student-to-student parcel delivery platform designed to make parcel collection easier within campus.

Students often receive parcels when they are attending classes, busy with work, or unable to reach the collection point. CampusCarry connects these students with other students who are already travelling around campus and can collect and deliver their parcels.

How It Works

1. Create Pickup Request

2. Provide Details and Set Payment Amount

3. Delivery Partners View Request

4. Delivery Partner Accepts Request

5. Parcel is Collected and Delivered

Why CampusCarry

Convenient

Students can get their parcels collected without having to leave their classes, hostel, or other activities.

Student Powered

Delivery partners can help other students while they are already travelling around campus.

Verified Community

University email and student ID verification helps keep the platform limited to students on campus.

Earn While You Help

Students can accept delivery requests and earn a small amount for helping with parcel collection.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make campus parcel collection simpler, faster, and more convenient by using the existing movement of students across campus.
