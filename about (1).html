About CampusCarry

CampusCarry is a student-to-student parcel delivery platform designed to make parcel collection easier within campus. Students often receive parcels when they are attending classes, busy with work, or unable to reach the collection point. CampusCarry connects them with other students who are already travelling around campus and can collect and deliver the parcel for them.

How It Works

Create a Request

Pickup point, delivery location, parcel details and offered reward.

A Campus Runner Accepts

A student already moving around campus sees the request and accepts it.

Connect & Collect

Both students coordinate the handoff details through the platform.

Deliver & Earn

The Campus Runner delivers the parcel and receives the reward.

REWARDS & COMMISSION

You set the reward. The runner keeps almost all of it.

CampusCarry takes a flat 2.5% platform commission — the rest goes straight to the student doing the delivery.
