FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

CampusCarry is built for students of the BML Munjal University community. Any student can ask for a parcel to be collected, and any student can accept requests when they are already walking across campus.

CampusCarry is intended for university students, so accounts are meant to be tied to a student's university identity. Verification is not active in this prototype yet; it will be handled by the backend in a later phase of the project.

The student who needs the parcel sets the reward when creating the request. Runners see the reward before they accept. CampusCarry keeps a flat 2.5% platform commission and the rest goes to the runner.

Yes, as long as the parcel has not been picked up yet. Once a runner has collected the parcel, or after it has been delivered, the request can no longer be cancelled.

CampusCarry is designed so that the student who made the request and the runner who accepted it can see the full parcel details. Other students browsing requests only need the locations and the reward to decide whether to accept.
